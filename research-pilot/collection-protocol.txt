EXPLORATORY TRAINING COLLECTION — VERSION 3, 18 OCTOBER 2026

Dataset: commons-lang-exploratory-training-20261018-v3
Phase: exploratory_training. Efficacy eligibility remains false.

This protocol prepares real longitudinal data, not an already trained model.
The v2 technical calibration remains immutable and is not imported into v3.
It observed 316 classes: 313 PASSED, 3 ABORTED and no FAILED class. There were
91,987 successful leaves, 9 aborted leaves and 7 skipped leaves. Under the v2
conservative aggregation, an aborted descendant makes a nonfailed class ABORTED.
Consequently v2 supplied zero complete binary builds and zero positive labels.

The three calibration-exposed classes below are excluded from a NEW exploratory
policy/training panel before observing outcomes on the new upstream source:
  org.apache.commons.lang3.time.FastDateFormat_PrinterTest
  org.apache.commons.lang3.time.FastDateParser_TimeZoneStrategyTest
  org.apache.commons.lang3.time.FastDatePrinterTest
These exclusions are post hoc with respect to the v2 calibration. They narrow
the population and prevent a confirmatory or whole-suite efficacy claim. The
reason is the existing conditional JDK/locale assumptions, not an observed model
advantage. ABORTED is never relabelled PASSED or FAILED. The exact name-based
exclusion is fixed; every other discovered class, including future new classes,
belongs to the panel. New aborts make the panel incomplete; no new exclusions
are selected to obtain a fit. A future scope change needs a separately versioned
dataset. Coverage and panel sizes are reported for each revision.

The full original JUnit suite still runs and all original outcomes, leaf counts,
durations, publication markers and ingestion times remain in the raw evidence.
The panel event stream used for the frozen policies has a panel-only oracle.
Excluded class observations are retained separately with actual publication and
ingestion times. A panel-complete flag is not full known-leaf coverage and is not
proof of all possible dynamically generated cases. Timings include observation
and fsync overhead. JUnit controls actual execution order. Policies stay in shadow
mode and do not change the suite or its class order.

c89cdf682fe21b0c39759cf58186ed5336a105d4. This revision already existed before

Collector/fork SHA and upstream source SHA/tree are separate metadata fields.

Collection requires a clean checkout and an ancestor source commit. Immediately

and fetch provenance are retained in the source audit.

Subsequent primary samples are distinct newly selected upstream revisions on the
same declared first-parent lineage, in chronological selection order. The v3
training cohort remains exploratory; a confirmatory comparison requires a new
future observation window and separately frozen evaluation protocol. Any skipped
revision and its reason must be recorded before inspecting its test outcomes.
Source ancestry is checked before collection. A repeated upstream SHA, a workflow
retry, or a change only to collector files is not another independent build.
Retries may diagnose a prior observation and retain their own attempt metadata;
they do not inflate the primary sample count. Historical backfill, if needed,
must be identified as replay rather than presented as prospective history.

Run manually on the research branch. No schedule or unbounded batch is installed.
Preserve actual failed primary runs: workflow success reports collection validity,
not absence of test failures. Timeouts, incomplete panels and collection defects
are retained and reported but do not count as complete training builds. Do not
repeat one green revision 50 times or inject artificial failures to open a gate.

HISTORY, TARGET AND ENVIRONMENT

History must come from verified immutable v3 artifacts in the authorized fork,
matching the frozen collector/configuration signature, original feature hashes,
source lineage and actual publication/ingestion availability. Closed observations
enter later decisions only when genuinely available at the cutoff. Original
features are never recomputed using later labels. Technical calibration and
synthetic checks stay outside the new research history.

The target remains raw_failed at the declared class-panel level. This measures a
raw JUnit class failure, not a reproduced defect, nonflaky failure, vulnerability
or security incident. The frozen scorer rejects the reproduced_under_protocol
target. Such a target would require a separately implemented frozen diagnosis
pipeline with independent same-source reruns, comparable environments and a
matching failure cause. A diagnostic retry never silently replaces a raw label.

The environment scope is Ubuntu 24.04, Temurin 21, Python 3.12 and one JUnit class
slot with parallel execution disabled and pinned scoring dependencies. Actual
JDK, image, architecture and dependency versions are recorded. Do not silently
pool an incompatible JDK/OS/harness change; document compatibility prospectively
or create a separate cohort. Compilation and synthetic probes stay outside
policy preparation; measured preparation and callback reserve remain explicit.

FIT READINESS — FROZEN MODEL UNCHANGED

The model implementation and its recorded SHA256 remain unchanged. A candidate
refit needs at least 50 available prior COMPLETE binary-panel builds, at least
20 raw failed class-execution rows in its last 200 complete builds, and both
binary labels in each chronological partition. These are 20 positive class
rows, not 20 failing builds. Split by build: first floor(0.6*n) for fitting,
next floor(0.2*n) for selecting C, remainder for calibration. No shuffled split.

Refits occur every 10 decision indices. Between them the original scheduled
refit is reconstructed at its own cutoff using its original frozen snapshots;
reconstruction cost is measured. With no excluded or unavailable builds, index50
is the earliest candidate using 50 earlier builds. Incomplete panels, rare
failures, missing labels in a temporal partition or numerical fit failure can
postpone or prevent fitting. A data-readiness report does not itself fit a model.
Until all gates pass and fitting succeeds, LOGIT retains its frozen B4 fallback.
No training date or comparative improvement is promised. Zero real positives
cannot support logistic fitting, calibration or a failure-recall comparison.

After each collection, retain the full evidence and the data-only readiness
report: unique source count, complete/incomplete panel counts and reasons,
positive rows, last200 window, three partition counts, gaps and next scheduled
refit. Review failure prevalence and project feasibility at a prespecified batch
boundary rather than weakening gates after seeing outcomes. Expand to another
justified project only through a separately recorded protocol and environment.

Download and digest-verify every artifact before its configured 30-day expiry.
Keep original ZIPs and manifests outside GitHub for the thesis. Cumulative JSON
history has a 1GB safety ceiling: reaching it stops collection rather than
truncating earlier samples. A larger archive design needs an explicit version.
